# Workspace direction

A personal desk suspended visually in a seamless neutral studio backdrop. No walls, windows, ocean, platform, or extra furniture. The desk and its recognizable objects carry the identity.

## Visual system

- Palette: daylight #f5f5f3, warm amber #caa477, after hours #17191e, oak #b08257, Space Black #25282c, Owala slate #353c4a (lid cream #dcd3bb, loop sage #627a69).
- The ambient slider interpolates the backdrop, key light, fill light, and ambient intensity. Foreground colors maintain readable contrast through intermediate settings.
- System sans-serif for the workspace; Apple system font stack in the desktop. Quiet introduction centered vertically on the left, with the workspace toolbar and mood slider docked beneath it; identity, introduction, dock, and footer share one left gutter and the same breathing room top and bottom. The interactive desk sits on the right, and a toggle beside the title collapses the identity, introduction, and dock to the left so the desk glides to the center. The footer carries the location and time, the drag hint in the middle, and the sign-off. On phones the desk sits between the introduction and entry controls.
- Refined original procedural models: white standing-desk legs, oak top, Space Black MacBook Pro with notch, ports, keyed keyboard with Touch ID, trackpad, and the Apple mark on its lid, curved ultrawide display, a Magic Keyboard with Touch ID (wedge body, 78 relief keys in the compact layout, legends), a Magic Mouse (tapered glossy shell on an aluminium base with the mark), gray/white Sayl suspension chair with chrome base, AirPods Max in Midnight on a silver stand (knit canopy between slim arms, chrome stems and collars, squircle anodised cups with knit cushions, Digital Crown and button), Fredy's own Owala FreeSip modelled from his photos (slate body with the vertical wordmark, tan collar with the embossed wordmark, cream dome lid, white spout cover with hinge springs, flat sage loop, coral push button), off-white ceramic coffee mug, and a desk-height controller box that is a miniature of the keypad with a live seven-segment readout.
- Materials combine soft lighting with metal reflections, fabric/plastic roughness, wood grain, and contact shadows. No external HDRI or 3D asset service is required.

## Arrival and interactions

1. Animated fm. wordmark while the client scene, texture, and shaders become ready.
2. Four-second camera glide past the chair and accessories. The monitor wakes near the end.
3. The camera settles and the workspace interface appears.

Reduced motion bypasses the camera animation. Session storage remembers a completed intro; a returning visit waits only for readiness. A stalled or failed renderer exposes the HTML desktop, with a server-rendered portfolio available without JavaScript.

The camera can be dragged, the desk height set from a dark keypad controller (LED readout, hold-to-move arrows, savable presets), and the mood adjusted continuously. Clicking the monitor transitions to the desktop. The headphones are decorative; a music player is deferred until the tracks and playback behaviour are specified.

## Desktop and content

Tahoe-inspired Finder chrome, translucent menus and Dock, traffic-light controls, custom app icons, light/dark appearance, draggable windows (the opening window is centred between the menubar and the Dock on screens wider than 720px), and keyboard search. The room's monitor shows the same desktop, painted sharp and kept in step with the mood and the language. The Dock starts with a house that returns to the studio, and the Finder face opens a window listing every item. This is a website interface, not a running copy of macOS. The whole site reads in English or Spanish: a small EN/ES pill at the top-right of the room and in the Display dropdown crossfades the copy.

The supplied 1200 × 2596 black wallpaper is stored unchanged at `public/images/pro-wallpaper-portrait.jpg` and rotated clockwise at display time. The desktop and device screens crop proportionally to fit.
