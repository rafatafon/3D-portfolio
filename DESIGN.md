# Workspace direction

A personal desk suspended visually in a seamless neutral studio backdrop. No walls, windows, ocean, platform, or extra furniture. The desk and its recognizable objects carry the identity.

## Visual system

- Palette: daylight #f5f5f3, warm amber #caa477, after hours #17191e, oak #b08257, Space Black #25282c, Owala blue #3c86a1.
- The ambient slider interpolates the backdrop, key light, fill light, and ambient intensity. Foreground colors maintain readable contrast through intermediate settings.
- System sans-serif for the workspace; Apple system font stack in the desktop. Large, quiet introduction on the left and the interactive desk on the right. On phones the desk sits between the introduction and entry controls.
- Refined original procedural models: white standing-desk legs, oak top, Space Black laptop with notch and ports, curved ultrawide display, gray/white Sayl suspension chair with chrome base, black headphones on a silver stand, blue Owala bottle.
- Materials combine soft lighting with metal reflections, fabric/plastic roughness, wood grain, and contact shadows. No external HDRI or 3D asset service is required.

## Arrival and interactions

1. Animated fm. wordmark while the client scene, texture, and shaders become ready.
2. Four-second camera glide past the chair and accessories. The monitor wakes near the end.
3. The camera settles and the workspace interface appears.

Skip is available throughout. Reduced motion bypasses the camera animation. Session storage remembers a completed/skipped intro; a returning visit waits only for readiness. A stalled or failed renderer exposes the HTML desktop, with a server-rendered portfolio available without JavaScript.

The camera can be dragged, the desk height changed, and the mood adjusted continuously. Clicking the monitor transitions to the desktop. Clicking the headphones currently shows a listening-corner note; Spotify playback is deferred until the playlist and playback behavior are provided.

## Desktop and content

Tahoe-inspired Finder chrome, translucent menus and Dock, traffic-light controls, custom app icons, light/dark appearance, draggable windows, and keyboard search. This is a website interface, not a running copy of macOS.

The supplied 1200 × 2596 black wallpaper is stored unchanged at `public/images/pro-wallpaper-portrait.jpg` and rotated clockwise at display time. The desktop and device screens crop proportionally to fit.
